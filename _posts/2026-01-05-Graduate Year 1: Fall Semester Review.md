<p>Last week, I finished my final project for the Big Data Systems course. Time flies—I’ve already spent a full semester at Tsinghua University. Due to special circumstances, my dormitory is located off-campus, but I am lucky enough to have a double room all to myself.</p>

<p>The courses at Tsinghua are quite challenging. Almost every course requires a final team project, which was quite a heavy lift for me. Since my supervisor only recruited two students in my major (Big Data), while most others are from AI, I had to find teammates from other students randomly. Unfortunately, I ended up being the leader of every group—some were assigned by TAs and others were decided by a roll of the dice. Consequently, I spent a significant amount of time managing these projects and completing homework. I also took 10 credits of Computer Networks and Distributed Systems, which were the most demanding courses of all. </p>

<p>Anyway, I have successfully completed all my courses for the first semester. I can now devote all my energy to research in the coming semester. My goal is to publish my first paper as the lead author. Good luck to me for ECCV 2026!</p>
